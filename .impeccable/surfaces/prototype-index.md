# Surface brief · prototype/index.html (landing B2B)

Scope: landing page prototype (static HTML/CSS/JS) to validate concept and identity. Visitor mode: **Persuade**.
Audience: clinic managers, independent doctors, receptionists (primary); patients join a waitlist (secondary).
Action: clinic pre-registration (form → n8n webhook). Proof: the mechanism itself (Solicitação Verificada, Modo Encaixe), shown with labelled synthetic data. No testimonials, logos, metrics or prices invented.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is a wayfinding route: a coral guide line, like the colored floor stripes in a hospital, leads the clinic from "agenda com buracos" to "cadastrar". Refuses the category default (smiling-doctor photo, blue gradient, three equal feature cards).
OWN-WORLD: Parede #F4F6FA ground, azul-noite #14254A sign plates and contrast bands, coral-pulso #FF5A3C guide line and primary actions, amarelo-sinal #FFC93C only on guide lines and alerts. No green except the "Confirmado" status. Signika headings, Atkinson Hyperlegible Next text. Components read as hospital signage: plates with arrows, numbered stops on a line, room-tag pills.
STORY: In seconds the clinic understands: new patients who already know you accept their plan; pay only per verified request; refill cancellations with Modo Encaixe; CFM and LGPD respected. Then it starts the registration.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Left: headline "Sua agenda cheia. Sem mensalidade.", one-paragraph offer, a working two-field start of the registration (clínica + WhatsApp) and the reassurance line. Right: the front-desk panel at work, a verified request arriving (KV-7K2Q) with accept/propose actions and a day strip where a freed 15h40 slot gets refilled. The coral guide line leaves the hero and becomes the "Como funciona" route.
FORM: own direction (launcher unavailable), seed: none (unavailable). Signature interaction: Modo Encaixe simulator (cancel → waves of notifications → confirmed).
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
